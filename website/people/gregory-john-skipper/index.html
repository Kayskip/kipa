Gregory John Skipper

Passed away 1 April 2018, aged 59

Whakapapa

Son of Ira Skipper and Montrose, father of Neha Skipper-Jones and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones, husband of Sharron Skipper, son of Albert Skipper through his mother Ira Skipper.

Bio

Gregory John Skipper was also known as Skip, and as Nga Karu O Te Rangi. He named his son Nga Karu O Te Rangi.
