Gregory John Skipper

Passed away 1 April 2018, aged 59

Ngāti Tawhirikura

Bio

Gregory John Skipper was also known as Skip.

Whakapapa

His mother was Ira Skipper and his father was Montrose. Albert Skipper was Ira's husband. He was the father of Neha Skipper and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper, and the husband of Sharron Skipper. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
