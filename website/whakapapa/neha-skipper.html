Neha Skipper-Jones

Profile

Whakapapa

He is the son of Gregory John Skipper and Sharron Skipper, and the brother of Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones. His grandmother was Ira and his grandfather was Montrose. Albert Skipper was his grandfather through his grandmother Ira. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
