Gregory John Skipper

Profile

Whakapapa

His mother was Ira and his father was Montrose. He was the father of Neha Skipper-Jones and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones, and the husband of Sharron Skipper. Albert Skipper was his father through his mother Ira. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
