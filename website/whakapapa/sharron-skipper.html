Sharron Skipper

Profile

Whakapapa

She is the daughter of Brian Jones and Molly Jones, the mother of Neha Skipper-Jones and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones, and the wife of Gregory John Skipper. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
