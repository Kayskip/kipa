Neha Skipper

Ngāti Tawhirikura

Bio

Neha Skipper is named for the ancestor Neha Kipa, and he is not that ancestor.

Whakapapa

He is the son of Gregory John Skipper and Sharron Skipper, and the brother of Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper. His grandmother was Ira Skipper and his grandfather was Montrose. Albert Skipper was Ira's husband. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
