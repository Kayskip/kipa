Sharron Skipper

Ngāti Tawhirikura

Whakapapa

She is the daughter of Brian Jones and Molly Jones, the mother of Neha Skipper and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper, and the wife of Gregory John Skipper. Gregory's mother was Ira Skipper and his father was Montrose. Albert Skipper was Ira's husband. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
