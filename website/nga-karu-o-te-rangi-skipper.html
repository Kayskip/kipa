Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper

Born 4 April 1999

Ngāti Tawhirikura

Bio

Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper, also known as Karu, is the son of Gregory John Skipper and Sharron Skipper, and the brother of Neha Skipper.

Whakapapa

He is the son of Gregory John Skipper and Sharron Skipper, and the brother of Neha Skipper. His grandmother was Ira Skipper and his grandfather was Montrose. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with marae Katere ki te Moana at Waiwhakaiho.
