Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper

Born 4 April 1999

Ngāti Tawhirikura

Bio

Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper is also known as Karu.

Whakapapa

He is the son of Gregory John Skipper and Sharron Skipper, and the brother of Neha Skipper. His grandmother was Ira Skipper and his grandfather was Montrose. Albert Skipper was Ira's husband. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
