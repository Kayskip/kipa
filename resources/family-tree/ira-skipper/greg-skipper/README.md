# Greg Skipper

Gregory John. Also Skip. Son of [Ira Skipper](../README.md) and [Montrose](montrose.md). Passed away on 1 April 2018, aged 59.

His [2018 notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/gregory-skipper-obituary?id=42229410) calls him son of Albert and Ira Skipper. His mother is Ira Skipper. His father is Montrose. Albert was Ira Skipper's husband.
