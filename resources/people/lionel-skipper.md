# Lionel Skipper

Sourced from [Rangi Kipa, 26 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html). The surname is the family name Rangi was using for his brothers. The comment says "my brother Glen and Lionel".

## Record

Youngest of the four siblings Rangi names. Son of Bert. Rangi said Lionel came along when Rangi was about ten, so about ten years younger than a brother born in 1966. Rangi left Lionel's own story for Lionel to tell. No comment from Lionel is on the [blog post](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) these notes use.
