# Mereana Kipa

Née Keenan. Sourced from [Tarina MacDonald, 2 April 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html).

## Record

Wife of Perere Neha Kipa, and mother of Eileen Te Ringa Hana (née Kipa). Tarina's line runs through that daughter to Julia Hana and then to Tarina.

In the [same comment](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) Tarina says she has history and pictures of "Fred and Mereana (Little Nana and Big Nana to us in Waikawa)". She does not say Fred is Perere. See [Fred](fred.md). Little Nana and Big Nana are not assigned to either name.

## Relationships

- Husband, as the parents of Eileen: [Perere Neha Kipa](perere-neha-kipa.md)
- Daughter: [Eileen Te Ringa Hana](eileen-te-ringa-hana.md)
- Named beside [Fred](fred.md), who may or may not be Perere

## Not established

Her parents. Whether she is Little Nana or Big Nana. Dates.
