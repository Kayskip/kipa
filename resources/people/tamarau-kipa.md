# Tamarau

Full name, as Rangi Kipa wrote it on 26 March 2010: Te Hekerangi a Tamarau. Commonly Tamarau.

## Record

Son of Rangi and Julie, younger than Kaiwhetu. Rangi, writing in March 2010, said he was 9 as of 26 February that year. He spent his early years at Smart Road with Nanny kuikui Phyllis, until Phyllis died. In 2010 he was living at Ohope Beach.

## Relationships

- Father: [Rangi Kipa](rangi-kipa.md)
- Mother: [Julie Paama-Pengelly](julie-paama-pengelly.md)
- Sister: [Kaiwhetu](kaiwhetu-kipa.md)
- Cared for in early childhood by [Phyllis](phyllis.md)

## Not established

A birth date beyond the age Rangi gave. A surname he uses.
