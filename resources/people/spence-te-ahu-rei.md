# Spence Te Ahu Rei

Sourced from Bonita Bigham (2018). She calls him her grandfather.

## Record

Husband of Apihaka. They lived at Waiokura Pā, Manaia, South Taranaki. Father of Hinewaito. His own father was Te Ahu Te Rei.

## Relationships

- Wife: [Apihaka](apihaka.md)
- Father: [Te Ahu Te Rei](te-ahu-te-rei.md)
- Daughter: [Hinewaito](hinewaito.md)

## Not established

Other children. Dates. His hapū, beyond the Waiokura residence.
