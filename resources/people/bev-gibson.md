# Bev Gibson

Née Robinson. Sourced from [her own comments, 27 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html), and from the [Te Ātiawa hapū page](https://teatiawa.iwi.nz/hapu-and-marae/nga-hapu-te-atiawa-hapu/ngati-tawhirikura/).

## Record

Matāmua of Mandy and Ihaka Robinson's children. She traces herself to Neha and Ngārere through Mandy, Dave Ngaiwi Skipper, and Mack Skipper. Mack was a son of Neha and Ngārere. She wrote the [comments](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) to put that line on the [reunion blog](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html), and said she did not know the Skipper whānau as well as she wanted, because her grandfather had been whāngai to Okaiawa.

Siblings, in the order she gives: Raewyn, David, Capree, Lee-ana, and Patrick, who is adopted. She counts three sisters and two brothers. Lee-ana is treated here as a sister. Her first comment is looser about that list. The second comment is the one these notes follow.

Married to Nigel, who prefers Gibby. Children, as she names them: Tami, David, Lane, and Sera. In one comment she calls them adult children, and in the other she is writing around Tami's wedding to Brad Cave. Four grandsons in 2010: Flynn and Max, who are Tami's, and twins Marley and Tate, who are her son David's. She was hoping for a granddaughter.

She is a registered nurse and was no longer working in hospitals. She had contracts with quality and health auditing agencies and with ACC. She was part owner of Beauty Treats, a beauty therapy clinic in New Plymouth, with her son Lane and the Robinson Whānau Trust. She held governance roles, had a master's thesis on the governance and management of Māori health providers, and was looking at PhD work.

Rangi Kipa wrote that, the week before [24 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html), he had been moko'ing Bev, Raewyn Ashby, and Lee-ana Robinson.

The [Te Ātiawa hapū page](https://teatiawa.iwi.nz/hapu-and-marae/nga-hapu-te-atiawa-hapu/ngati-tawhirikura/), as it stood in November 2026, listed Bev Gibson among the trustees of Ngāti Tawhirikura.
