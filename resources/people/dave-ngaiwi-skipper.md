# Dave Ngaiwi Skipper

Sourced from Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010.

## Record

Second son of Mack and Nganiwhi Skipper. Bev's grandfather. She places him with Te Ātiawa, Ngāti Rahiri, and Pukeariki hapū.

He was whāngai to kui Roopu and koro Mohi Moeahu at Okaiawa, and was raised at Aotearoa marae on Hastings Road. Bev says that whāngai whānau taught him te reo and tikanga, and that he had a privileged upbringing there.

He married Polly Whatumaramara. Their daughter is Mandy Te Hauoterangi Robinson (née Skipper).

Bev describes him as a big man with a gentle manner. She wrote in March 2010 that it was 27 years since they had laid him to rest, which places his death at about 1983.
