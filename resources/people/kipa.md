# Kipa

Sourced from the [Barrett Honeyfield page](https://barretthoneyfield.com/2019/02/21/ngati-te-whiti/), 21 February 2019. This is the ancestor, not a modern namesake, and not the name of this repo.

## Record

Son of Hineone, who was born in 1780 and was a daughter of Tautara. Father of Neha Te Manihera (Skipper), whom the page places with Ngāti Tawhirikura.

No spouse, dates, or other children are given.

## Relationships

- Mother: [Hineone](hineone.md)
- Grandfather, on this page: [Tautara](tautara.md)
- Son: [Neha Kipa](neha-kipa.md)
