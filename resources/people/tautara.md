# Tautara

Sourced from the [Barrett Honeyfield page](https://barretthoneyfield.com/2019/02/21/ngati-te-whiti/), 21 February 2019. This is a lead from another family's research, not a whānau manuscript.

## Record

Described there as a rangatira of Ngāti Tawhirikura, and elsewhere on the same page as a rangatira of Ngāti Rāhiri. He spent some time at Rewarewa pā, the Ngāti Tawhirikura pā on the northern bank at the mouth of the Waiwakaiho River. His usual residence is given as Nga-puke-turua pā (Puketapu), a few miles north of the Waiwakaiho. The page says his grandfather was Potaka Taniwha.

Father of Hineone, born in 1780, and of Kuramai-i-te-ra. Through Hineone, the page makes him the ancestor of Kipa and of Neha Te Manihera (Skipper).
