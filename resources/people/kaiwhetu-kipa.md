# Kaiwhetu

Full name, as Rangi Kipa wrote it on [26 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html): Te Rangi Kaiwhetu. Commonly Kaiwhetu.

## Record

Daughter of Rangi and Julie. Matāmua of those two children. Rangi, writing in March 2010, said she was 13 as of 27 December just past. She spent her early years at Smart Road with Nanny kuikui Phyllis, until Phyllis died. In 2010 she was living at Ohope Beach, and Rangi said the children were involved in surf lifesaving.
