# Hineone

Sourced from the Barrett Honeyfield page, 21 February 2019.

## Record

Daughter of Tautara. Born in 1780. Sister of Kuramai-i-te-ra, who was the mother of Wakaiwa Rāwinia. The page says Hineone was the mother of Kipa, whose son was Neha Te Manihera (Skipper) of Ngāti Tawhirikura.

## Relationships

- Father: [Tautara](tautara.md)
- Sister: [Kuramai-i-te-ra](kuramai-i-te-ra.md)
- Son: [Kipa](kipa.md)
- Grandson, on this page: [Neha Kipa](neha-kipa.md)

## Not established

Hineone's mother. Kipa's father, if he was someone other than an unnamed partner. Any confirmation from a Kipa whānau source.
