# Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones

Also Karu. Surname Skipper-Jones. Son of Greg Skipper and Sharron Skipper. Born 4 April 1999.

## Record

Named with his brother as a child of Gregory John Skipper in the [2018 death notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/gregory-skipper-obituary?id=42229410). [The notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/gregory-skipper-obituary?id=42229410) writes him as Karu, and is signed Jonesie, Neha and Karu. His father was also called Nga Karu O Te Rangi, and named him Nga Karu O Te Rangi.
