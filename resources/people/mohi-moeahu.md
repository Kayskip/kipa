# Mohi Moeahu

Sourced from Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010. She writes "koro Mohi Moeahu".

## Record

Whāngai father of Dave Ngaiwi Skipper, with kui Roopu, at Okaiawa. Dave was raised at Aotearoa marae, Hastings Road. Bev says that whāngai whānau taught him te reo and tikanga.

His hapū beyond the Okaiawa and Aotearoa marae setting Bev gives. Any tie of his own to Neha and Ngārere.
