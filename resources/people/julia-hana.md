# Julia Hana

Sourced from [Tarina MacDonald, 2 April 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html).

## Record

Only daughter of Eileen Te Ringa Hana (née Kipa) and Tari Hana, who lived at Waikawa Bay, Picton. Mother of Tarina MacDonald.

## Relationships

- Parents: [Eileen Te Ringa Hana](eileen-te-ringa-hana.md) and [Tari Hana](tari-hana.md)
- Daughter: [Tarina MacDonald](tarina-macdonald.md)

## Not established

Her spouse. Other children. Dates. A married name. Tarina says "my mother was Julia Hana" and does not give another surname.
