# Eruera Te Puke Mahurangi

Kept so he is not folded into the Kipa line.

## Record

On the Barrett Honeyfield page he is the father of Wakaiwa Rāwinia Barrett. The same page calls Kuramai-i-te-ra Rāwinia's mother, so the two are her parents there. A census of 1847 is cited for Eruera, Rāwinia, Kararaina, and Hera as Ngāti Te Whiti. Other papers cited there call him a chief of Ngāmotu.

A chat summary turned this into "Eruera Kipa (Skipper), son of Neha". That person is not on the page. Do not carry the link unless another source names him.

## Relationships

- Daughter, on that page: Wakaiwa Rāwinia Barrett. She is not given a file here.
- Partner, as the parents of Rāwinia on that page: [Kuramai-i-te-ra](kuramai-i-te-ra.md)

No relationship into the Skipper or Kipa lines is established.
