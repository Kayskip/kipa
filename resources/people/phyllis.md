# Phyllis

Sourced from [Rangi Kipa, 26 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html). No surname is given.

## Record

Rangi calls her Nanny kuikui Phyllis. Kaiwhetu and Tamarau spent their early years with her at Smart Road, until she died. By March 2010 she had already died. He does not say whose mother she was, or which side of the family she belonged to.

## Relationships

- Looked after [Kaiwhetu](kaiwhetu-kipa.md) and [Tamarau](tamarau-kipa.md), the children of [Rangi Kipa](rangi-kipa.md) and [Julie Paama-Pengelly](julie-paama-pengelly.md)

## Not established

Her whānau. Her surname. A passing date.
