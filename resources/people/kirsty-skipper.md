# Kirsty Skipper

Also Ngāmata, and Ngamata in Bev Gibson's spelling. Rangi calls her Kirsty. Bigham and a photograph credit use Ngāmata Skipper.

## Record

Sister of Rangi Kipa, the next child after him. Daughter of Bert. Rangi, writing in March 2010, said she followed not long after his own birth in 1966.

Bev Gibson wrote that, apart from Rangi, Ngamata (Kirsty), and a handful of others, she did not know her Skipper whānau as well as she wanted.

Bigham records that in 2011 Rangi completed moko kauae on his sister Ngāmata at the papakāinga, Kātere ki te Moana, with whānau and friends around them. Four other women received moko kauae the same day. The photograph is courtesy of Ngāmata Skipper.
