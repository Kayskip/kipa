# Eileen Te Ringa Hana

Née Kipa. Sourced from [Tarina MacDonald, 2 April 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html).

## Record

Daughter of Perere Neha Kipa and Mereana Kipa (née Keenan). With Tari Hana, she lived at Waikawa Bay, Picton. Their only daughter was Julia Hana. Tarina MacDonald is Julia's daughter, so Eileen is Tarina's grandmother.

## Relationships

- Parents: [Perere Neha Kipa](perere-neha-kipa.md) and [Mereana Kipa](mereana-kipa.md)
- Partner: [Tari Hana](tari-hana.md)
- Daughter: [Julia Hana](julia-hana.md)

## Not established

Other children. Dates. Tarina says Julia was their only daughter, which leaves open whether there were sons.
