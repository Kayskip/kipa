# Sharron Skipper

Wife of Greg Skipper. Daughter of Brian Jones and Molly Jones.

## Record

Named with Greg in [Ira Skipper's 2020 notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/ira-skipper-obituary?id=39917700), in the same pair form as Ray and Jan: "Greg (dec) and Sharron". [Greg's 2018 notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/gregory-skipper-obituary?id=42229410) calls him her husband, and son-in-law of Brian (deceased) and Molly Jones. Mother of Neha Skipper-Jones and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones.

The [2018 notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/gregory-skipper-obituary?id=42229410) is signed Jonesie, Neha and Karu. Jonesie is not identified further.
