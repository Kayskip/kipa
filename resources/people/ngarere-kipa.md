# Ngārere Kipa

Wife of Neha Kipa. The [reunion blog](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) writes her name Ngarere.

## Record

Sourced from [Bonita Bigham (2018)](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861):

- Ngāti Tawhirikura, Te Ātiawa.
- Lived with Neha at Kātere ki te Moana, Tārereari papakāinga, above the Waiwakaiho River.
- Mother of 11 children, including Apihaka.
- A weaver, kairaranga, working harakeke. She had her own pā harakeke. Some whānau still had bushes that whakapapa back to it when Bigham was writing.
- Hinewaito, her granddaughter, remembered her always making something, usually functional: a kawe or a kete for the garden or for kaimoana. The kete were functional and still woven to a high standard. There was always new raranga around her whare.
- Bigham is kaitiaki, through her mother Hinewaito, of one kawe Ngārere used to carry wood from the beach about 2 km below the papakāinga. The [exegesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861) dates that kawe at about 1930, and includes a whānau photograph of Ngārere.
- Hinewaito does not remember seeing Ngārere do tāniko.
- Bigham also describes her as renowned for her gardening, managing many acres of crops, and treats Glen Skipper's later māra kai at Kātere as continuing that practice.

The chat called her née Kihi. That name is not in the [blog](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html), the [Barrett page](https://barretthoneyfield.com/2019/02/21/ngati-te-whiti/), or the [exegesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861).
