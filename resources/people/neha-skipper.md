# Neha Lee Skipper-Jones

Middle name Lee. Surname Skipper-Jones. Son of Greg Skipper and Sharron Skipper. Named for the ancestor [Neha Kipa](neha-kipa.md). Not the same person as that ancestor.

## Record

Named as a child of Gregory John Skipper in the [2018 death notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/gregory-skipper-obituary?id=42229410), with his brother. The notice writes him as Neha, and writes his brother as Karu.
