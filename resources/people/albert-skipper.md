# Albert Skipper

Also Mammy. Husband of Ira Skipper.

## Record

Died about February 2010. Rangi Kipa, writing on 24 March 2010 to descendants of Neha and Ngārere, recorded Uncle Albert Skipper's tangi that February at Katere ki te Moana / Tarereare.

Ira's 2020 notice calls her the wife of the late Albert (Mammy).
