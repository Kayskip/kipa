# Albert Skipper

Also Mammy. Husband of Ira. Her surname is not confirmed.

## Record

Died about February 2010. Rangi Kipa, writing on [24 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) to descendants of Neha and Ngārere, recorded Uncle Albert Skipper's tangi that February at Katere ki te Moana / Tarereare.

[Ira's 2020 notice](https://deaths.taranakidailynews.co.nz/nz/obituaries/taranaki-daily-news-nz/name/ira-skipper-obituary?id=39917700) calls her the wife of the late Albert (Mammy).

## Relationships

- Wife: [Ira](../profiles/active/ira.md). Her surname is not confirmed. Family record, 12 November 2026.
- Father of Gregory John Skipper through Gregory's mother Ira, and grandfather of Neha Skipper-Jones and Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones through their grandmother Ira. Family record, 12 November 2026.
- Called uncle by [Rangi Kipa](rangi-kipa.md)
