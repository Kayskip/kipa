# Bert Skipper

Sourced from Rangi Kipa's comment, 26 March 2010. Surname taken from the Skipper name Rangi says he changed. The comment itself says "my Father Bert".

## Record

Father of Rangi, Kirsty, Glen, and Lionel. When Rangi was born in 1966, Bert was in Christchurch training as a carpenter at Te Kaihanga.

Rangi calls John his own great-grandfather. Bert is Rangi's father, so Bert's parent, the child of John, is not named.

## Relationships

- Children, in the order Rangi gives: [Rangi Kipa](rangi-kipa.md), [Kirsty Skipper](kirsty-skipper.md), [Glen Skipper](glen-skipper.md), [Lionel Skipper](lionel-skipper.md)
- Ancestor, with a generation missing: [John Skipper](john-skipper.md)

## Not established

His parents. His spouse. A surname he himself used. Dates.
