# Tarina MacDonald

Sourced from her comment on the reunion blog, 2 April 2010.

## Record

Daughter of Julia Hana. Julia was the only daughter of Eileen Te Ringa Hana (née Kipa) and Tari Hana of Waikawa Bay, Picton. Eileen was the daughter of Perere Neha Kipa and Mereana Kipa (née Keenan).

She wrote as one of the uri of Neha and Ngārere, thanking Rangi for the blog and saying the Waikawa family had documented information, history, and pictures of Fred and Mereana, Little Nana and Big Nana, which she would post. She had met Ngamata in Wellington a few years earlier. When she wrote, she had moved to the Gold Coast in Australia for a while. No later comment from her is on the post these notes use.

A trustee named Te Rina MacDonald was listed for Ngāti Tawhirikura on the Te Ātiawa hapū page in November 2026. These notes do not treat Te Rina and Tarina as the same person.
