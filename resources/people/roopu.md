# Roopu

Sourced from Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010. Bev writes "kui Roopu". No surname is given.

## Record

Whāngai mother of Dave Ngaiwi Skipper, with koro Mohi Moeahu, at Okaiawa. Dave was raised at Aotearoa marae, Hastings Road. Bev says that whāngai whānau taught him te reo and tikanga.

## Relationships

- Whāngai son: [Dave Ngaiwi Skipper](dave-ngaiwi-skipper.md)
- With: [Mohi Moeahu](mohi-moeahu.md)

## Not established

Her hapū and parents. Whether she and Mohi were a couple beyond sharing this whāngai. Any tie of her own to Neha and Ngārere.
