# Mandy Te Hauoterangi Robinson

Née Skipper. Sourced from Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010.

## Record

Daughter of Dave Ngaiwi Skipper and Polly Whatumaramara. Mother of Bev Gibson. Married Ihaka Robinson of Maketu, Te Puke, Te Arawa.
