# Greg Skipper

Gregory John. Also Skip. Son of Ira Skipper.

## Record

Died at Taranaki Base Hospital on 1 April 2018, aged 59. A private service had already been held at the family home. Notice published in the Taranaki Daily News on 7 April 2018.

Husband of Sharron. Father of Neha and Karu. Son-in-law of Brian Jones and Molly Jones.

The notice calls him son of Albert and Ira. His mother is Ira. Albert was Ira's husband.
