# Perere Neha Kipa

Sourced from [Tarina MacDonald, 2 April 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html). Placed with Neha and Ngārere only as far as her comment allows.

## Record

Tarina addresses the uri of Neha and Ngārere, then gives her line. Eileen Te Ringa Hana (née Kipa) was the daughter of Perere Neha Kipa and Mereana Kipa (née Keenan). She does not explicitly say Perere was a son of Neha and Ngārere. The name is consistent with that, and it is not stated.

In the [same comment](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) she speaks of documented information, history, and pictures of "Fred and Mereana". She does not say Fred and Perere are the same man. See [Fred](fred.md).

## Relationships

- Wife, as Tarina gives it: [Mereana Kipa](mereana-kipa.md)
- Daughter: [Eileen Te Ringa Hana](eileen-te-ringa-hana.md)

## Not established

His parents. Whether he is Fred. Whether he is one of the 11 children of Neha and Ngārere.
