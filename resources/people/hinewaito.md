# Hinewaito

Sourced from [Bonita Bigham (2018)](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861). No surname is given. Bigham's mother.

## Record

Born in 1927, daughter of Apihaka and Spence Te Ahu Rei. Granddaughter of Neha and Ngārere. Mother of Bonita Bigham, born 1971.

She remembered her grandmother Ngārere always weaving, usually something to be used: the kawe, or kete for the garden and for kaimoana. She did not remember Ngārere doing tāniko. She remembered Ngārere's kete as functional and finely made, and new weaving always around the whare.

She grew up watching Apihaka do tāniko and did not learn it from her. She learned later from a friend from Waikato, more than 30 years after Apihaka died. When Bigham showed her early tāniko and said she loved the work, Hinewaito told her about Apihaka and gave her pieces Apihaka had made.

Bigham is kaitiaki, through Hinewaito, of Ngārere's kawe and of Apihaka's tāniko pieces.

Hinewaito made a kākahu huruhuru from contemporary materials. It took more than 10 years of work on and off. Bigham says she was an active member of Te Rōpū Raranga Whatu. Bigham grew up watching that cloak being made, and learned whatu herself from Puhi Nuku and Ria Waikerepuru rather than from her mother.
