# Te Ahu Te Rei

Sourced from Bonita Bigham (2018).

## Record

Father of Spence Te Ahu Rei. Bigham says he was a staunch supporter of the Parihaka movement, as Neha Kipa was. She also says Waiokura and Kātere were often the last stopovers, southbound and northbound, for people travelling to Parihaka.

No spouse, dates, or other children are given.

His parents, spouse, or hapū. Any tie to Neha and Ngārere other than the shared Parihaka connection Bigham describes.
