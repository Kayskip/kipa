# Julie Paama-Pengelly

Rangi Kipa's wife in the records used here. He names her Julie. Bigham gives the fuller name.

## Record

Rangi wrote on [26 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) that he and Julie married at the mouth of the Wai-o-Ngona on New Year's Eve 1998, which is when he took the surname Kipa. He credits her with suggesting a blog rather than a paid website. Their children are Kaiwhetu and Tamarau. In 2010 they were living at Ohope Beach.

Bigham, writing about the late 1990s when she lived in Rangi's New Plymouth home, calls Julie his wife at that time: Julie Paama-Pengelly of Ngāi Te Rangi, a multi-discipline artist and fine arts teacher. Bigham says the two of them shaped her sense of visual art as a carrier of cultural knowledge.

The [2010 blog](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) still speaks of Julie as his partner and as the children's mother. These notes do not go past that.
