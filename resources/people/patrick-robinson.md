# Patrick Robinson

Sourced from Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010.

## Record

Younger brother of Bev, and the last in the sibling order she gives. She marks him as adopted. In the first comment he is "a younger adopted brother, Patrick", added after she has named Raewyn, David, Capree, and Lee-ana. In the second he is inside the order: Raewyn, David, Capree, Leeana, and Patrick.

## Relationships

- Adoptive parents, as Bev's account places him in this sibling set: [Mandy Te Hauoterangi Robinson](mandy-robinson.md) and [Ihaka Robinson](ihaka-robinson.md)
- Siblings: [Bev Gibson](bev-gibson.md), [Raewyn Ashby](raewyn-ashby.md), [David Robinson](david-robinson.md), [Capree Robinson](capree-robinson.md), [Lee-ana Robinson](lee-ana-robinson.md)

## Not established

His birth parents. Dates. Bev does not spell a surname for him. Robinson is used here because she places him in the Robinson sibling set.
