# Apihaka

Sourced from [Bonita Bigham (2018)](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861). No surname is given.

## Record

A daughter of Neha and Ngārere, one of their 11 children. A tāniko weaver. Bigham is kaitiaki of the few tāniko pieces still known to have been made by her, including headbands the [exegesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861) dates to about the 1920s.

She married Spence Te Ahu Rei and lived with him at Waiokura Pā, Manaia, South Taranaki. She was the mother of Hinewaito, born 1927.

Hinewaito grew up watching her do tāniko, and did not learn the art from her. Hinewaito later learned from a friend from Waikato, more than 30 years after Apihaka died. The [exegesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861) does not give the year of death.
