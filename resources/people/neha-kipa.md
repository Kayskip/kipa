# Neha Kipa

Also recorded as Neha Te Manihera (Skipper). Ancestor the [reunion blog](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) was gathering descendants for. Not the same person as [Neha Skipper-Jones](neha-skipper.md).

## Record

Sourced from [Bonita Bigham, _Haehae and the art of reconciliation_ (2018)](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861):

- She calls him her great-grandfather, of Ngāti Te Whiti, Te Ātiawa.
- A supporter of the Parihaka passive resistance. She says he was imprisoned, taken to Ōtākou (Dunedin) in 1869, and returned to Taranaki in 1872.
- He lived at Kātere ki te Moana, Tārereari papakāinga, above the Waiwakaiho River in Ngāmotu (New Plymouth), with his wife Ngārere.
- One of their 11 children was a daughter, Apihaka. The [exegesis](http://hdl.handle.net/10179/15861) includes a whānau photograph of him.
- Bigham says she does not know whether he was active in the arts.

Sourced from the [Barrett Honeyfield page](https://barretthoneyfield.com/2019/02/21/ngati-te-whiti/) (21 February 2019):

- Neha Te Manihera (Skipper) of Ngāti Tawhirikura.
- Son of Kipa, and through Kipa the grandson of Hineone.

The two hapū labels do not match. Both are kept. Neither has been checked against whānau kōrero.

The [reunion blog](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html) of 24 March 2010 addresses "nga uri o Neha raua ko Ngarere Kipa".

## Relationships

- Father, on the [Barrett page](https://barretthoneyfield.com/2019/02/21/ngati-te-whiti/): [Kipa](kipa.md)
- Grandmother, on that page: [Hineone](hineone.md)
- Wife: [Ngārere Kipa](ngarere-kipa.md)
- Children named in these sources: [John Skipper](john-skipper.md), [Mack Skipper](mack-skipper.md), [Apihaka](apihaka.md). [Perere Neha Kipa](perere-neha-kipa.md) is claimed only as far as Tarina's comment allows.
- Namesake of [Neha Skipper-Jones](neha-skipper.md)

## Not established

The other children among the 11. A surname "Kihi" for Ngārere. A son named Eruera Kipa. See [Eruera Te Puke Mahurangi](eruera-te-puke-mahurangi.md). Which of his children [Albert Skipper](albert-skipper.md) descends from.
