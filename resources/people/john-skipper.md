# John Skipper

Sourced from [Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html).

## Record

A son of Neha and Ngārere, and a brother of Mack. Bev calls him Rangi Kipa's great-grandfather. She states the brother relationship twice: "Mack and John (Rangi's great grandfather) were brothers", and "Rangi's greatgrandfather (John) and my greatgrandfather (Mack) were brothers."

## Relationships

- Parents: [Neha Kipa](neha-kipa.md) and [Ngārere Kipa](ngarere-kipa.md)
- Brother: [Mack Skipper](mack-skipper.md)
- Great-grandson: [Rangi Kipa](rangi-kipa.md)
- Descendant, with the intervening parent unnamed: [Bert Skipper](bert-skipper.md)

## Not established

His spouse. His children. The parent who links him to Bert. John is Rangi's great-grandfather and Bert is Rangi's father, so one generation between John and Bert is missing from these sources.
