# Mack Skipper

Sourced from [Bev Gibson, 27 March 2010](http://skipperwhanaure-union.blogspot.com/2010/03/e-te-iti-e-te-rahi-e-nga-puia-raukura-o.html).

## Record

A son of Neha and Ngārere. Bev calls him one of the boys, and one of several sons, of that couple. Brother of John, who was Rangi Kipa's great-grandfather. Husband of Nganiwhi. Bev's great-grandfather.

## Relationships

- Parents: [Neha Kipa](neha-kipa.md) and [Ngārere Kipa](ngarere-kipa.md)
- Brother: [John Skipper](john-skipper.md)
- Wife: [Nganiwhi Skipper](nganiwhi-skipper.md)
- Son: [Dave Ngaiwi Skipper](dave-ngaiwi-skipper.md), whom Bev names as the second son

## Not established

His other children. Nganiwhi's whānau. Birth or passing dates.
