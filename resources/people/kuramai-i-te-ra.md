# Kuramai-i-te-ra

Sourced from the Barrett Honeyfield page, 21 February 2019. She is in these notes because the page uses her to place Hineone.

## Record

Daughter of Tautara, and sister of Hineone. The page calls her Rawinia's mother, spelling the daughter's name Wakaiwa Rāwinia in the article title. Kuramai-i-te-ra is not herself described as a Kipa or Skipper ancestor.

## Relationships

- Father: [Tautara](tautara.md)
- Sister: [Hineone](hineone.md)

## Not established

Any descent from her into the Skipper or Kipa lines in these notes.
