## Whakapapa

He is the son of [Gregory John Skipper](greg-skipper.md) and [Sharron Skipper](sharron-skipper.md), and the brother of [Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper-Jones](nga-karu-o-te-rangi-skipper.md). His grandmother was [Ira Skipper](ira-skipper.md) and his grandfather was Montrose. [Albert Skipper](albert-skipper.md) was his grandfather through his grandmother Ira Skipper. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.
