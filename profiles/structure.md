# Profile structure

Website profiles are split into two folders. [Active](active) profiles are the people whose information is shown and can be opened. [Inactive](inactive) profiles are names held until the information is verified. An inactive name appears on the people list and does not open a page. Research notes stay in [resources](../resources/README.md). An active profile uses these fields, in this order.

- **Full name:** The name shown on the site.
- **Date of birth:** Write `<insert>` until it is known. Keep this off the home list.
- **Passed away:** The date, when this person has passed. Leave the line out when there is no passing to record. Keep this off the home list.
- **Iwi:** Ngāti Tawhirikura
- **Image:** A path to a picture. Write `<insert>` until the image is added.
- **Bio:** Who this person was, and what they did for the whānau. This is not the family link. Write `<insert>` until that account is known.
- **Family link:** This person's own relations, in this order: child of, parent of, partner of, sibling of. Separate them with commas. Name only the people this person is related to.
- **Whakapapa:** The family link, then the line those connections sit on. The marae is Katere marae at Waiwhakaiho.

```markdown
# Full name

**Full name:** <insert>

**Date of birth:** <insert>

**Passed away:** <insert>

**Iwi:** Ngāti Tawhirikura

**Image:** <insert>

## Bio

<insert>

## Family link

<insert>

## Whakapapa

<insert>
```
