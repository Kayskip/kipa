## Whakapapa

He is the son of Gregory John Skipper and Sharron Skipper, and the brother of Nga Karu O Te Rangi Skipper. He is named for the ancestor Neha Kipa, and he is not that ancestor. His grandmother was Ira Skipper and his grandfather was Montrose. The whānau is of Ngāti Tawhirikura, with marae Katere ki te Moana at Waiwhakaiho.
